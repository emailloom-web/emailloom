Get to know your coverage and ways to ramp it up
You can never be too protected, and FDIC insurance is your safety net in the unlikely event of bank failure. Today’s standard limit on FDIC insurance coverage is set at $250,000 per depositor, per insured bank, for each account ownership category.6 By opening different accounts using varying ownership categories, you can easily expand your coverage.
The following is a hypothetical example of how a married couple was able to secure FDIC insurance on $1.5 million in deposits at EverBank.
