Pick your team!

Once you reach level 5 and visit a Gym, you can choose to join one of three teams: Instinct (yellow), Mystic (blue), or Valor (red).

You'll stick with your team throughout your Pokémon GO journey, so choose wisely! Trainers from opposing teams battle for control of Gyms, which is reflected by the color of the Gym.

LEARN MORE

All about Berries

Berries can be used when encountering a wild Pokémon to make them easier to catch. There are three types of Berries to choose from.

Nanab Berries make it easier to land a Poké Ball throw by slowing down the Pokémon's movement. Reach level 4 to unlock this Berry.

Razz Berries make it less likely that the Pokémon will break out of the Poké Ball. Reach level 8 to unlock this Berry.

Pinap Berries double the amount of Candy you'll receive if you successfully catch the Pokémon. Reach level 18 to unlock this Berry.

And there's more!

Add friends

You can add friends in Pokémon GO to stay connected with Trainers you meet during your Pokémon GO journey. Each Trainer has a unique 12-digit Trainer Code and a corresponding QR code. You can use either of these codes to add a Trainer as a friend.

Connect with friends and explore the world together using Campfire.

Campfire can help Trainers connect, discover local communities, and find nearby Raid Battles. Trainers can choose to host or join a raid through Team Up.
